Concept A · Social object

An event page and a Home story are the same thing: a column of white cards on the app’s grey. Every card starts with a square and two lines, every action sits in one row at the bottom, and the event’s colours stay on its cover and its squares. Built the way Facebook keeps a lot of content calm: very few sizes, repeated exactly.

Type: four sizes, one family

13 / 20Meta: time, place, counts
14 / 20Body, names, buttons
16 / 24Card titles
26 / 32Event title

Tahoma only (the app’s --sys). No Helvetica, no mono, no uppercase kickers in content.

Space: 8 · 16 · 24 · 32

8
16
24
32
  • Card padding 16. 16 between cards (8 on a phone).
  • 16 between blocks in a card, 8 between rows of a list.
  • A square and its words are always 12 apart.

Squares: text matches them

  • 40 = 20 + 20. Every story, person and list row: square plus two lines.
  • 64 = 24 + 20 + 20. Every attachment (event, horizon, call out).
  • 32: replies and the face pile only. 160: the cover (96 on a phone).
  • The left edge is always the square’s edge. Nothing is nested to the right.

Actions: one row, always last

  • Hairline, then the app’s bevel buttons, 36 high, left-aligned.
  • Message, Answer, Follow and Share live there, never in a header.
  • Colour never reaches a control. Only the cover and the squares carry the look.

What changed on the event page

  • The lined facts grid is gone. When, where, entry and host are four icon rows in a Details card, followed by the description.
  • One font. The page used Helvetica Neue, Lucida mono kickers and Tahoma controls. Now it is Tahoma only, with sentence-case card titles.
  • The look is a cover, not a world. Colour is limited to the top card. Below it the page is the app’s white and grey, like Home.
  • Call outs are posts. Author, ask, Answer (private) and Share, with public replies under them and a reply box. With no call outs there is no card.
  • Who’s going shows faces. People you follow come first with their status, and everyone else is a pile with a count.
  • Timeline, Bring and Take part are one key/value card, with no dashed rules and no bullets in the accent colour.

What changed on Home

  • Nothing is nested to the right. Today the event card hangs off a 32px avatar column. Now the story’s object runs the full width, aligned to the avatar’s left edge.
  • One avatar size per role. 40 for whoever the story is about, 32 for replies. The 20px overlapping second avatar and the 18px faces are gone.
  • Every story is a card with the same anatomy: header (40 + two lines), optional text, a 64px attachment, then the action row.
  • Actions in one place. Message moved out of the header into the action row as “Message Rio”. Answer, Follow and Show more sit there too.
  • The call-out strip became a story, still inverted, at the same type sizes as everything else. It is no longer a 32px headline. Its public replies preview under it.
  • Day bands, the greeting and the mono labels are cut. Relative time on each story does that job.
