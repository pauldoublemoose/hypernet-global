Concept B — Document window

The event page and Home stop being posters pasted into the app and become documents in the same window: the app's grey face for chrome, white sheets for content, hairline group boxes for sections, and one font (Tahoma) at three sizes. An event's own colour lives only in its square and its title band. Home is one entry anatomy repeated — every actor at 32, every thing at 48, everything on an 8px grid.

  • Spacing 8 / 16 / 24 (4 only between buttons in a group). Type 12 / 14 / 20 in Tahoma, line-heights 16 (UI), 20 (prose), 24 (titles). No mono, no capitals.
  • Sizes 32 = a person doing something or listed · 48 = a thing (event, horizon, suggested person) · 96 = the page's own square (64 on phone).
  • Controls the app's bevel button at 36 (44 on touch); 28 only for an inline button inside a header line (Message).
