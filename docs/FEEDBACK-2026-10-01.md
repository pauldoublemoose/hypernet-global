# Feedback — 2026-10-01 (call with Sebastian + Michal's direction)

Source: call with Sebastian Östlind (Hypernet owner), 2026-10-01, and Michal's brief after it.
Goal of this round: **a v1 that is shippable** — simpler, fewer features, ready to be wired to Neon
(real email login, real database) once the owner says so. Nothing is wired to a backend in this round.

## The outcome v1 must deliver

1. **A person finds the right events to go to — through their network.** (primary)
2. An event creator finds co-creators through call outs. (secondary)

Every screen is judged on whether it serves (1). Expected scale: ~2,000 sign-ups/year, ~100 active
users. Usage is occasional ("what do I do next weekend?"), not daily — so Home must catch you up fast.

## Sebastian, from the call (paraphrased)

- Home should feel alive: "your friend Michael promoted this event", "Michael just published a horizon".
  **Recency is the signal** — "Raven marked going 2 hours ago" makes you reach out; 20 days ago doesn't.
- Old-Facebook style "Seb followed 5 new people" is how you discovered **friends of friends**; the home
  feed should prompt you to get to know friends of friends.
- The current categorical feed (sections) is valuable — it becomes **Explore**.
- **Call outs must be attention-demanding**: "someone stands up in a room and shouts". You shouldn't have to
  look for them; they should pop in the Home feed, and also be browsable under Explore.
- Horizons are central (the Spotify-playlist idea). Chats matter for trust ("you don't know me, but I want
  to join as a laser guy").
- Prefer self-explanatory UI over tutorial overlays; short descriptions for esoteric words ("Event Horizon").
- Customizer: too many dropdowns. Let people **pick their own colours** freely and **add an image**.
  Square image upload in v1; background images maybe later. He noticed the flickering scanlines/atmosphere.
- Groups (camp pages, post as group, group chats, announcements), notifications, themes/settings menu
  (black / white / silver / polychrome skins) → **version 2**.

## Interpretation — v1 scope

**In**
- **Home = network activity feed.** Reverse-chronological social signals from people you follow (and a little
  from friends of friends): going / interested / promoted an event, published or followed a horizon, added
  events to a horizon, created an event, posted a call out, followed a person. Relative time ("2h", "yesterday"),
  day bands. Each item lets you act on the event inline. Call outs pop visually. Friends-of-friends prompts
  appear in the stream. Your own actions in the session appear in the stream (as "You …") so it feels live.
- **Explore** = the old categorical Home, plus tabs: **Events** (list + calendar), **Horizons**, **Call outs**, **People**.
- Left nav: Home, Explore (+ its four kinds), then your things (Going / Interested / Hosting / Promoted,
  your horizons, followed horizons, people you follow). Search stays.
- **Chat = direct messages between two people only.** No new group chats.
- **Customize = colours + a square image.** Free colour pickers (background, ink/text, accent), one square
  image upload (cropped to a square), for **events, horizons and your profile**. Plain default stays.
- Images are kept in the browser for now (downscaled data URL); they move to real storage with Neon.

**Out (v2)**
- Groups / camp pages / posting as a group / group chats / announcements / posts.
- Settings menu and interface themes (black, white, silver, polychrome, Windows, terminal).
- Notifications. Background images. Canvas art options (backdrop, atmosphere, parallax, ornaments, type,
  headline, motion, surface, pattern) in the customizer.
- Tutorial overlays.
