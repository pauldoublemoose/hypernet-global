# What burn events actually publish: field reference and event schema

Research date: 2026-09-23. The facts come from official sites, participant guides and survival guides. Facts found only in third-party directories are marked as such. Every source is listed at the bottom.
Companion data: [`data/real-events.json`](../../data/real-events.json).

---

## 0. Corrections to our current assumptions

- **The Borderland is in Sweden, not Denmark.** It started in Sweden in 2011 and ran in Denmark from about 2015. In 2023 it returned to Sweden, to land the community bought at Alversjö near Eksjö. Prices are in SEK.
- **Nowhere no longer exists under that name.** Going Nowhere SL has stopped producing it. The same community ran **Elsewhere** on 7–12 July 2026 in the same Monegros desert. The new organiser is the non-profit Nobodies Collective.
- **Midburn is on and off.** It was cancelled in 2019, 2020 and 2023. It returned in 2024 and 2025, and 2026 is planned for 2–7 Nov ("Tzin City"). Those dates are provisional because sources disagree.
- **Czechia has no official regional burn.** There is a Regional Contact (Burning Man Czech Republic, Prague) and a community gathering called **Čarodění**. Čarodění is tied to Witches' Night (30 April) and its effigy is a Man/witch hybrid. The nearest official regional is **Schönburn** in Austria.
- **Fusion is not a burn.** It runs as a non-profit association (e.V.) with a ticket lottery, but it has paid bars and food stalls and never mentions Burning Man. Use it only as a contrast.
- **Most 2026 big burns are already over.** As of today the upcoming items are the Borderland membership sale (4–11 Oct), autumn decompressions (Zurich, SF/Oakland, London, Netherlands, Lithuania) and Midburn in November. The 2027 dates that are already announced are Burning Man, Borderland, AfrikaBurn, Burning Nest and Elsewhere (Elsewhere's are pending approval).

## 1. Field reference

For each field: what it is, why participants care, and real examples.

### When

- **Dates (main event)**
  - Why it matters: people book leave and travel.
  - Examples:
    - Burning Man 2026: gates open 00:01 Sun 30 Aug and close Mon 7 Sep.
    - Borderland: always ISO week 30 (20–26 Jul 2026; 26 Jul – 1 Aug 2027).
    - AfrikaBurn 2026: 27 Apr – 3 May.
- **Phases** (build, early entry, gates open, effigy burn, temple burn, strike or exodus)
  - Every big burn publishes a timeline, not a single date range.
  - Why it matters: the main way to take part is to arrive early and build, or stay late and strike.
  - Burning Nest 2026:
    - Build crew from 11 May.
    - Work Access Permits from 15 May.
    - General arrival 18 May.
    - Non-strikers leave by 15:00 on 25 May; strikers stay until 27 May.
  - Burning Man: the Man burns Saturday night before Labor Day; the Temple burns Sunday at about 20:00. Dates are "weather permitting".
  - Kiez Burn 2025: build 24–28 Jul, strike 4–6 Aug.
- **Early entry credential**
  - Burning Man calls it the Setup Access Pass (SAP), formerly the Work Access Pass. AfrikaBurn uses Work Access Passes (WAP). Burning Nest uses Work Access Permits.
  - They are handed out through camps, art projects and departments.
  - Why it matters: it shows how you get in early, and that you do it by joining a crew.
- **Gate hours and last entry**
  - Borderland's gate is open 09–22.
  - Burning Man closes entry at 18:00 on burn Saturday.
  - Small events: Zurich Decompression's gate closes at midnight Saturday. Kiez Burn nights say "last entry depends on door volunteers".
- **Recurrence** (small events): for example the Boulder Burner Meet-up, every 2nd Wednesday.
- **Next edition**
  - Pages announce the next year early. Burning Man 2027 is 29 Aug – 6 Sep with the theme "Spellbound". Borderland 2027 and AfrikaBurn 2027 are also announced.

### Where

- **Region and country**, plus a **site name** that becomes a proper noun:
  - Black Rock City (Burning Man)
  - Tankwa Town at Quaggafontein (AfrikaBurn)
  - Alversjö (Borderland)
  - "the Glade", a secret location near Newton Abbot, Devon (Burning Nest)
  - "Secret Forest", about an hour from Berlin (Kiez Burn)
- **Address visibility.** Many sites are secret until you have a ticket. Nest and Kiez Burn say this outright. Small events publish the venue: Kraftwerk Selnau in Zurich, The Loom in Oakland.
- **Land ownership.** Borderland's community owns 100 ha of forest and lakes. Burning Man runs under a BLM permit.
- **Distance and transport.** Examples: the Burner Express Bus ($159 from Reno), the Burning Nest bus from London Victoria (£27).

### Size

- **Population cap and last attendance.**
  - Burning Man: BLM permit cap of 80,000; the 2025 census estimate was 71,500.
  - AfrikaBurn: about 10,000.
  - Borderland: "over 5,000" by its own count; Burning Man's Sweden page says about 4,500.
  - Nowhere: about 2,670 in 2023; Elsewhere says about 3,000.
  - Kiez Burn: 1,500.
  - Vårlys (Norway): 217 in 2025.
  - Why it matters: size is the fastest signal of what kind of event it is.
- Small events almost never state capacity.

### Access (the biggest variation, and the one to get right)

- **What you buy differs from event to event:**
  - **Ticket:** Burning Man, AfrikaBurn, Nest, Elsewhere.
  - **Membership** of a non-profit association: Borderland (Gränslandet Ideell Förening), Vårlys (Norway), Schönburn (Austria).
    - It exists for legal reasons. The page says "membership", and there is no "buy ticket".
  - **Members-only tickets:** London Decompression. Membership is free, and tickets are sold only to members.
  - **Lottery:** Fusion (register in December, two draws). Borderland used to run a lottery; its current sale text does not mention one.
  - **Donation / free:** camp fundraisers and burner meetups.
- **Named tiers, framed as values rather than perks:**
  - **Burning Man 2026:** $550 / $675 / $775 / $975 / $1,500 / $3,000.
    - $775 "covers the cost per participant". Each tier has a line of copy, not a benefit.
  - **Burning Nest 2026:**
    - "subsidise me by £20": £200
    - "actual event price": £220
    - "showing Nest some love": £265
    - "Moar Art Plz": £285
    - "BEST ART EVER": £315
  - **Nowhere:** VIP means "Voluntarily Inflated Price" and comes with zero perks.
  - **Borderland 2027 memberships:** 3,000 / 2,222 / 1,234 SEK.
  - **AfrikaBurn 2026:**
    - Ground Up: R3,099
    - New Horizon: R4,999 (includes a donation)
    - Blue Sky: R7,199
    - Anathi: R299, for historically excluded groups
    - Subsidised: R999
    - Pensioner 70+: free
- **Low-income tickets.** Almost every event has them:
  - Burning Man Ticket Aid: $250, with a $75 vehicle pass.
  - Nest Inclusion ticket: £100.
  - Nowhere low-income: €100.
  - Zurich Decompression low income: CHF 45.
  - Kiez Burn: "cross-funded by the community".
- **Directed sales** (reserved for camps, art and infrastructure crews):
  - Burning Man's **Stewards Sale**, formerly the Directed Group Sale.
  - AfrikaBurn's **Direct Distribution** tickets: 1,400 reserved for creative projects.
- **Sale windows.** These are events in their own right, and people plan around them:
  - Burning Man: Sunrise Sale (Feb), Stewards Sale (Mar–Apr), Main Sale (Apr 29 – May 8), OMG Sale (Jul 29 – Sep 4), STEP resale (Mar–Aug).
  - Borderland: fall sale 4–11 Oct 2026 (non-transferable); spring sale from 7 Feb 2027 (transferable).
  - AfrikaBurn: "First Flame", then "Second Strike".
  - Elsewhere: price waves from €275 to €315.
- **Transfer rules.** Resale runs only through official channels: STEP (Burning Man), Quicket (AfrikaBurn, Nowhere), members.theborderland.se, face value only (London). The name on your ID must match the ticket (AfrikaBurn, Elsewhere, Kiez Burn, Borderland).
- **Vehicle pass:**
  - Burning Man: $165.
  - AfrikaBurn: "E-Toll", an environment toll per motor vehicle.
  - Nest: £25 parking permit.
  - Nowhere: none needed.
- **Fees and payment plans.** Burning Man adds Nevada Live Entertainment Tax at 9% plus fees, and offered payment plans for the first time in 2026. AfrikaBurn offers Payflex instalments.

### Who can come

- **Age:**
  - Burning Man: 12 and under free (a Kid's Ticket is still needed); under 18 needs a guardian over 21.
  - Borderland: 0–13 free and registered, with a kids' curfew.
  - AfrikaBurn: 13 and under free; minors must be linked to an adult aged 28 or over.
  - Vårlys: minimum age 20, but children up to 6 may come.
  - Fusion: 18+.
  - SF/Oakland Decompression: all ages, but 21+ indoors after 21:00.
- **Pets.** Banned almost everywhere:
  - Burning Man: "no-dogs event since 2003".
  - Nowhere, Kiez Burn, Nest (assistance dogs need pre-approval), AfrikaBurn (service dogs only), Vårlys.
- **Non-participants.** Borderland runs a guided public art walk for non-members. Most events have no spectator option at all ("not a spectator sport").

### Culture and principles

- **Principles.** Most events use the 10 Principles, often with local changes:
  - The Burning Man 10: Radical Inclusion, Gifting, Decommodification, Radical Self-reliance, Radical Self-expression, Communal Effort, Civic Responsibility, Leaving No Trace, Participation, Immediacy.
  - Borderland adds **Consent** as a "+1".
  - Burning Nest renames LNT **Leave A Better Trace**, adds AfrikaBurn's **Each One Teach One**, and makes Consent the **"0th Principle"**.
  - Nowhere wrote its own 10 (No Commerce, Community, Cooperation…) and adds Consent.
  - Kiez Burn has 11, ending with Consent.
  - **Pattern:** Consent is effectively the 11th principle across Europe.
- **Theme for the year:**
  - Burning Man 2025 "Tomorrow Today", 2026 "Axis Mundi", 2027 "Spellbound".
  - AfrikaBurn 2026 "Through The Prism".
  - Burning Nest 2026 "Not Actual Size".
  - Amber Burn 2026 "Circus Mundus Absurdus".
  - Midburn 2025 "Let There Be Light!"
  - Zurich Decompression 2026 "The Grand Hotel for Peculiar Burners".
  - **Not every event has a theme.** Borderland, Elsewhere and Kiez Burn publish none, so the field is optional.
- **Effigy and temple.** The burnable centrepieces are named:
  - The Man (Burning Man).
  - The San Clan (AfrikaBurn).
  - The Effigy on Saturday and the Temple on Sunday (Nest).
  - 2026 BRC Temple: "Temple of the Moon".
- **Local vocabulary.** Render it as-is and don't translate it. See section 3.

### Participation (what makes it a burn and not a festival)

- **Camps.** Each event has its own word for them:
  - "theme camps" (Burning Man, Nest)
  - "barrios" (Nowhere/Elsewhere)
  - "Kieze" (Kiez Burn)
  - "camps / clusters" (Borderland)
- **Camp numbers and rules:**
  - Burning Man places more than 900 camps.
  - Midburn 2025 had about 200.
  - Borderland caps a camp or cluster at 1,250 m².
- **Registration windows.** Nest closed 15 March. Burning Man's Statement of Intent leads into Stewards Sale access.
- **Camp listings carry tags.** Examples: "accepts new members", "open to others", "kids/family friendly", "common food", "sound camp".
  - These are exactly the hooks for Hypernet Clusters and Call outs.
- **Art funding.** Three different models:
  - **Juried grants:**
    - Burning Man Honoraria: about 70–75 projects per year from a budget of about $1.3–1.45M. 2026: 75 projects, $1.3M.
    - Nowhere/Elsewhere: up to €5,000, typically €500–3,000, as reimbursement.
    - Nest: Small under £100, Medium £100–1,000, Large £1,000–2,000; 70% paid in advance.
    - AfrikaBurn Creative Grants: materials only.
  - **Participatory budgeting through Borderland Dreams:**
    - Members hand out **real money** to projects over three capped rounds (20%, then 50%, then no cap).
    - Funding is all-or-nothing against each project's minimum.
    - 2026 pot: 3.69M SEK, plus Fund 33 (1.82M SEK) for large or permanent art.
    - It runs on Cobudget. Kiez Burn also uses the word "Dreams".
  - **Spark / community grants** off-site: AfrikaBurn has 11 Spark Grants, one per principle.
- **Volunteering.** Expectations differ:
  - **Required:** Vårlys (you pick a role when you buy); Elsewhere ("every single ticket holder is expected to volunteer").
  - **Strongly encouraged:** Borderland (first-timers are asked to do a Clown Police shift); Nowhere (FIST system; a shift of 6 hours or more earns a meal at La Cantina).
  - **Voluntary and unmonitored:** Burning Nest.
  - **Named teams:** Gate / Threshold, Rangers / Nomads / Clown Police, Welfare / Sanctuary / Welfare Enough / Care Bears, LNT, DPW, Lamplighters, B.O.W.E.L.S / ShitNinjas (toilets).
  - **Tools:** Borderland's membership platform ("Reality Shifts"), signup.com (Kiez Burn), the Humans app (Elsewhere).
- **Mutant vehicles / art cars.** They must be licensed:
  - Burning Man's DMV (Department of Mutant Vehicles): separate day and night licences, 5 mph limit.
  - Borderland's "Department of Muddy Vehicles".
  - Nowhere "Artomobiles".
  - AfrikaBurn DMV: 10 km/h, and e-bikes over 400 W must be "mutated".

### Rules and self-reliance

- **Decommodification / no vending.** Nothing is sold, with one exception that is always ice:
  - Burning Man sells ice at Arctica.
  - AfrikaBurn and Nowhere sell only ice.
  - Borderland, Nest and Kiez Burn sell nothing.
  - **Small city events differ:** bars are often paid (Kiez Burn nights run a "cashless bar"). Fusion has paid bars.
- **What's provided and what isn't.** Participants check this first:
  - **Water:**
    - Burning Man provides none; bring 1.5 US gal per person per day.
    - AfrikaBurn provides none; bring at least 5 L per person per day.
    - Nowhere barrios buy it in 1,000 L cubes at €50 each.
    - Borderland has well-water taps and a power grid.
    - Nest and Kiez Burn have drinking-water taps.
    - Vårlys has one tap.
  - **Toilets:** portaloos or compost toilets are provided. **Showers:** none, almost everywhere.
  - **Food:** never provided, except volunteer kitchens and camp "common food".
- **Leave No Trace / MOOP.**
  - Burning Man calls itself "the largest LNT event in the world".
  - Most events have no bins: pack it in, pack it out.
  - Kiez Burn and Vårlys ban glitter, feathers and confetti.
  - Borderland and Nest reframe it as **Leave a Better Trace**.
- **Vehicles on site.** Mostly no driving:
  - Borderland: one-hour unloading slots on 18–20 Jul, then long-term parking.
  - Kiez Burn: parking about 500 m from the gate.
  - Nowhere: one car pass per barrio, 10 km/h, and cars cannot move during the event.
  - Nest: no bikes or e-scooters.
- **Fire, sound and drones:**
  - Fire:
    - Borderland: a 6 kg extinguisher per camp.
    - Nowhere: total ban on solid fuel.
    - Kiez Burn: fire bowls only, at least 50 m from the forest.
  - Sound: zones or registration above a threshold. Borderland: over 1 kW. Kiez Burn: louder than a Bluetooth speaker. AfrikaBurn: graded in 3 levels.
  - Drones: banned (Nowhere, Nest, AfrikaBurn), or by permit only (Burning Man).
- **Banned items.** Burning Man prohibits fireworks, firearms and weapons, and can turn back the whole vehicle at the Gate.

### Safety and care

- **Consent.** Burning Man's definition is "freely given, reversible, informed, enthusiastic, specific".
  - Borderland has a 24/7 Consent Team and consent drop boxes.
  - Nest uses the FIRES checklist.
  - Vårlys: "Only YES means YES!"
  - Kiez Burn: RAD (Radical Accountability Division) with a harm report form.
- **Care teams:** Rangers, Welfare, Sanctuary, Safer Use space, ESD / medics, Helm (sober on-call leads).
- **Emergency.** Kiez Burn asks people *not* to call 112 and to use its on-site paramedics instead.
- **ID at gate.** Photo ID must match the ticket or membership: Borderland, AfrikaBurn, Kiez Burn.

## 2. Small events: what they look like

Most Hypernet events will be small. These are the real patterns found (full examples in `real-events.json` and in the sources).

- **Decompression ("decom")**
  - A one-night or one-weekend city party after the burn, often a mini-burn.
  - Examples:
    - Zurich: 9–11 Oct 2026 at Kraftwerk Selnau, CHF 45 / 65 / 85. Friday build, Saturday event, Sunday strike brunch.
    - SF/Oakland: 17 Oct 2026 at The Loom, $35–80.
    - London: 7 Nov 2026, members only.
    - Dutch decompression, Amsterdam, 8 Nov 2025: Radion club, build Fri–Sat, event 20:00–06:00, strike 06:00–10:00.
- **Burn Night / precompression / reunion**
  - Kiez Burn's BurnHalla: 31 Jan – 1 Feb 2026, noon to noon, €15–55, potluck, ticket sales close 24 hours before.
  - Frühlings Burn Night: €15, bring your own dishes, contributions claimed on a shared spreadsheet.
- **Build weekend / build day / Tool School**
  - The BRC Temple 2026 crew ran build weekends every Saturday at an Oakland warehouse. Unskilled volunteers were welcome and brought their own PPE; 319 volunteers took part.
  - Borderland (Alversjö) reportedly holds monthly work weekends coordinated on Discord (not verified).
- **Camp fundraiser**
  - Donation entry, DJs, and proceeds named. Example: a Kiez Burn sound camp's funds went to turntables and transport (not verified).
  - Usually on Resident Advisor or Partiful.
- **Camp meeting / camp call / Spark campout**
  - Kiez Burn's "Spark Campout" lets leads visit the site before the event.
  - Camps such as Curious Creatures (Elsewhere) run regular camp calls. Its dues are €275 for infrastructure plus €100 for food.
- **Town hall / meetup / Stammtisch**
  - Portland Town Hall: free, hybrid, ASL interpreting.
  - Boulder Burner Meet-up: every 2nd Wednesday, drop-in, free.
  - Zurich burners: the 10th of every month.
- **Service days**
  - Burners Without Borders park cleanups.
  - First-timer orientations, some on Zoom.

**What small-event listings carry:**
- date and time, often overnight, split into build / event / strike blocks
- venue, often a club, warehouse or community space
- price tiers: low-income / standard / supporter, or donation, or free
- a sale cutoff
- a volunteer shift link, with "volunteers get in free" or "volunteers still need a ticket"
- open calls for art, DJs and workshops
- a principles or code-of-conduct link
- a theme and a costume note
- potluck or bring-your-own-cup
- age split (all ages by day, 21+ at night in the US)

**What small-event listings usually lack:** capacity, a survival guide, early entry, vehicle rules, and no-vending rules (bars are often paid). The real detail sits in an FAQ, a newsletter, or a Telegram or Discord channel.

**How people find them:**
- Facebook groups and events (private groups act as the gate)
- Telegram and Discord
- mailing lists
- ticket shops: pretix, Stager, Eventfrog, Eventbrite, Resident Advisor
- Partiful as a social RSVP layer (the SF decompression showed 290 going and 306 interested)
- the official Burning Man event finder. It carries only name, type (Regional / Decompression / BM Project), dates, location, a short description and a link.

## 4. Proposed Hypernet event schema

The schema extends the existing `HyperEvent` fields (`id, title, date, endDate, startTime, endTime, timezone, description, externalUrl, hostName, ownerIds, ownerGroupIds, privacy, venueKind, venue, recurrence`). It keeps their names and adds nested optional blocks.

Legend:
- **R** = required for every event
- **O** = optional on any event
- **S** = optional, mostly used by small events
- **B** = only in the `burn` block, which is rendered when `scale` is `"burn"`, or `"gathering"` with a block present

### Core (all events)

- `id` R
- `title` R
- `kind` R: `burn | decompression | burn_night | build | fundraiser | camp_meeting | meetup | town_hall | workshop | volunteer_day | sale | other`
  - `sale` covers ticket and membership sale windows. People follow them like events.
- `scale` R: `burn` (multi-day, gated site, usually 1,000+) | `gathering` (multi-day or overnight, roughly 50–1,000) | `meetup` (hours).
  - This chooses the page layout.
- `date` R (start, `YYYY-MM-DD`)
- `endDate` O
- `startTime` O
- `endTime` O
- `timezone` R (IANA)
- `recurrence` O: `{ text: "2nd Wednesday monthly", rrule? }`
- `venueKind` R: `physical | virtual | hybrid`
- `city` R for physical events when public. Rural or secret burn sites may give only `region` / `country` (for example Čarodění, Schönburn).
- `region` O
- `country` R (ISO-2)
- `venue` O: `{ name, address?, addressVisibility: public | on_rsvp | ticket_holders | secret }`
- `hostName` R
- `ownerIds` R
- `ownerGroupIds` O
- `organiser` O: `{ name, legalForm?, url? }`, for example `"Gränslandet Ideell Förening"` or `"Kiez Burn e.V."`
- `description` R: one or two sentences
- `privacy` R: existing enum
- `externalUrl` O
- `access` O:
  - `model`: `free | donation | pay_what_you_can | ticket | membership | members_only | lottery | invite`
  - `currency`
  - `tiers[]`: `{ name, price, note?, quantity? }`
  - `lowIncome`: `{ price?, note }`
  - `sales[]`: `{ name, opens, closes?, note? }`
  - `transfer`: `none | face_value | official_platform`, plus `transferVia?`
  - `idMustMatch` (bool)
  - `vehiclePass`: `{ price, note }`
  - `asOf`: the year the prices refer to
  - `note`: free text, for example "no gate sales" or "volunteers get in free"
- `age` O: `{ min?, kidsFreeUpTo?, note? }`
- `principles` O: `{ base: "10 Principles" | "own" | null, additions[]: ["Consent", "Each One Teach One"], renamed[]?, url? }`
- `theme` O: `{ name, blurb? }`
- `volunteering` O: `{ expectation: none | encouraged | expected | required, how?, teams[]? }`
- `calloutIds` O: links to Hypernet Call outs, for example "build crew needed" or "DJs wanted"
- `openCalls` S: `["art", "DJs", "workshops"]`
- `whatToBring` S: for example `["own cup", "potluck dish", "PPE"]`
- `dressCode` S: a free-text costume note
- `gateCloses` S: last entry, for example `"Sat 24:00"`
- `blocks` S: `[{ label: "build" | "event" | "strike", date, startTime, endTime }]`
  - This is the small-event version of phases. For example, Zurich runs Fri build / Sat event / Sun strike brunch.
- `accessibility` O: free text
- `sources` R for real events: `[{ url, label }]`
- `illustrative` O (bool): `true` for synthetic fixtures
- `unverified` O: list of field paths that come only from third-party sources

### `burn` block (B, big events only)

- `edition`: `{ year, number? }`
- `nextEdition`: `{ date, endDate, theme?, note? }`
- `phases[]`: `{ kind: build | early_entry | gates_open | effigy_burn | temple_burn | gates_close | strike, date, endDate?, time?, note? }`
- `population`: `{ cap?, lastAttendance?, lastAttendanceYear?, note? }`
- `regionalStatus`: `flagship | official_regional | burn_inspired | independent`
- `site`: `{ name, terrain?, ownership? }`
- `earlyEntry`: `{ credential: "SAP" | "WAP" | "Work Access Permit", via: "camps, art, departments" }`
- `camps`: `{ noun: "theme camps" | "barrios" | "Kieze" | "camps", registration?: { closes }, count?, maxSize? }`
- `artFunding`: `{ model: honoraria | grants | dreams_cobudget, name, pot?, currency?, range?, note? }`
- `effigies`: `[{ name, burns: "Sat night" }]`
- `provided`: `{ water: none | taps | buy, power: bool, toilets, showers: bool, food: none | volunteer_kitchen, note? }`
- `rules`: `{ vending: "none" | "ice only" | text, vehicles, artCars, pets, fire, sound, drones, other[] }`
- `safety`: `{ consentTeam: bool, teams[] }`
- `vocabulary`: `[{ term, meaning }]`: local terms shown in a glossary strip

### Rendering rules

- **`meetup`:**
  - Show title, time, venue, price or donation, host and Cluster, "what to bring", call outs, then the three verbs.
  - Never render empty "burn" sections.
- **`gathering`:** add `blocks`, `access.tiers`, `volunteering`, and the principles link.
- **`burn`:** add the phases timeline, the access explainer, "provided / bring your own", rules and safety, camps and art funding, and vocabulary.
- **Sale windows** (`kind: sale`, or `access.sales`) should show up in horizons as their own dated items. For example: "Borderland membership sale — 4–11 Oct".

## 5. Design findings (what surprised us)

1. **"Buy ticket" is the wrong primary action for a lot of burns.**
   - Borderland, Vårlys and Schönburn sell a **membership** in a non-profit association. London Decompression is **members-only**. Fusion uses a **lottery**.
   - Tier names carry values ("subsidise me by £20", "Moar Art Plz", "Voluntarily Inflated Price") and give no perks.
   - The access area should explain the model: what you're joining, when the sale window is, who gets in during which phase. It should not be a price button.
2. **The event is a timeline, not a date.**
   - Build (1–3 weeks) → early entry (by credential, through a camp or art crew) → gates → effigy / temple burns → strike.
   - The build and the strike are where co-creators actually meet. So "join the build" is the natural Hypernet hook, and it maps directly to Call outs.
3. **Sale windows are events in themselves.**
   - Burning Man runs five named sales. Borderland's non-transferable fall sale runs 4–11 Oct 2026.
   - People plan their year around these. An Event Horizon should be able to hold them.
4. **Participation is built from local nouns.**
   - Camps are barrios or Kieze. Funding is Honoraria or Dreams (member-allocated real money: 3.69M SEK in 2026). Shifts are Realities or FIST.
   - Store the host's own terms (`camps.noun`, `artFunding.name`, `vocabulary`) and render them verbatim. Generic labels would read as tourist copy.
5. **"What's provided" is the practical core of a burn page, and it varies a lot.**
   - Water ranges from none (Burning Man: 1.5 gal/day; AfrikaBurn: 5 L/day) to paid cubes (Nowhere) to well-water taps (Borderland).
   - Vending is none or ice only. Pets are banned nearly everywhere. Consent is effectively principle 11.
   - Small events show almost none of this. Their core is time, venue, price and volunteers, and much of their detail lives in chat. Hence the `scale` switch.
