Concept B2 — Windows

Home is one centred column. Opening an event opens it in its own window over the feed — drag it by the title bar, double-click the title bar or press □ to maximise, × or Esc to close. One thing at a time: opening another event replaces what the window shows. On a phone the window is a full-screen sheet with × in its title bar, and closing it puts you back in the feed where you were. Call outs keep Answer, and an answer is public, under the call out.

  • Try it: drag the event window; open another event from the feed (click its title); press Answer on the call out, type, post; Esc closes.
  • Rhythm as in B: spacing 8 / 16 / 24 · type 12 / 14 / 20 in Tahoma · 32 = a person, 48 = a thing · controls 36 (44 touch).

Notes

One window, not many. A second window would mean comparing two events side by side — rare here, and it brings back the clutter the owner wants gone. Opening another event replaces the window's content; the feed row that is open stays marked.

Where it opens. Over the right of the feed, so the feed's left edge — avatars, who did what, the call out's Answer — stays visible beside it. It remembers where you dragged it until you close it.

Answer is public. Answer on a call out (Home or event) takes you to the box under the call out; what you post shows there for everyone, with your name and time. No direct messages from a call out.
